Welcome to **Study Blog**! 👋

This is my personal knowledge hub where I document my technical journey, write study notes, and share insights on software engineering, web development, and computer science.

## What to Expect Here

- 📖 **Study Notes:** Detailed notes on programming languages, algorithms, and system design.
- 💡 **Guides & Tutorials:** Step-by-step walk-throughs of modern web technologies, frameworks, and tools.
- 🛠️ **Project Showcases:** Real-world projects, architectural decisions, and experiments.
- 🎯 **Tips & Best Practices:** Quick solutions, performance optimization tips, and developer workflows.

## Technology Behind This Blog

This site is powered by:
- **[Astro](https://astro.build/):** For fast, content-driven static site generation.
- **[Svelte 5](https://svelte.dev/):** For interactive components and widgets.
- **[Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/):** For elegant, responsive layouts.
- **[Pagefind](https://pagefind.app/):** For instant offline search across all posts.
